About

Most of my career has been spent in commerce systems — the kind where inventory, pricing, and orders have to stay in sync across a handful of platforms that disagree with each other. I like the integration-heavy, correctness-sensitive end of the work.

Professionally that's meant Ruby on Rails, TypeScript, and AWS. On my own time it's mostly Rust: developer tooling, a few proxies, and the occasional microcontroller soldered into a household object that never asked for one.
